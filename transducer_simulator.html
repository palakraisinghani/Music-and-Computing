Ready

Pitch = machine speed ÷ instructions per loop. A tight loop that pulses the speaker every 4 instructions on a 1,000 instr/s machine gives 250 Hz; stretch the loop to 8 instructions and it drops to 125 Hz. At true early-1950s speeds the tones are low and buzzy — raise the speed to hear them as clearer notes. The music programs (6–8) use music mode: at 16,000 instr/s a loop can be ~100 instructions long and still be audible, and long loops can be tuned finely enough to land on real notes. SLEEP leaves the speaker silent, so the gaps between bursts of loops become rhythm. Speeds are approximate; verify them before citing.
