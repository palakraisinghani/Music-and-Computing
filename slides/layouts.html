1843 · Note A

Music as a Formal System

Lovelace saw that the engine's real subject was not number but the relations between symbols, and music gave her the clearest proof.

  • A finite alphabet: twelve pitch classes, a handful of durations
  • Explicit rules: harmony, counterpoint, metre
  • So composing becomes a search through legal combinations

Moore School, Philadelphia

Reprogramming Meant Rewiring

ENIAC had no stored program. Its "program" was a physical arrangement of patch cables and rotary switches, and changing the problem could take days of replugging.

Twenty accumulators, ten decimal digits each: about 200 digits of working state in total.

1951 → 1957

One Bit versus Many

The 1-bit speaker

  • Pitch comes only from timing
  • Always a square wave: fixed, buzzy timbre
  • One note at a time
  • Real time, because keeping time is its whole job

The DAC

  • Any waveform, computed sample by sample
  • 4,096 levels from 12 bits
  • Any number of voices, any timbre
  • But every sample must be calculated first

From playing the machine like a metronome to asking it to calculate the sound itself.
