Every swara is a loop. One trip round the loop clicks the speaker once, so a loop of L instructions sounds at speed ÷ L Hz. Sa is set to C4. Each loop length below is the whole number of instructions closest to the true pitch, over three saptaks (C3–C6). A faster machine means longer loops, and longer loops can be tuned more finely.

Raag scales, alankars and a tihai, written in sargam. Press play to hear them as loops, or open one in the editor below to change it.

2Compose with sargam blocks

Each cell is a swara block and shows its loop size. Click a cell to add it to your composition. A dot below means mandra, a dot above means taar, an underline means komal, and a bar above Ma means tivra.
